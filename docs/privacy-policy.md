# Time Breaker Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-31

Time Breaker is a break reminder for macOS. It doesn't collect, sell, or share personal data.

## Data on your Mac

- **Settings.** Your break schedule, appearance, and language settings are stored on your Mac in the app's sandboxed preferences.
- **Background images.** If you choose a background image, the app stores a private copy in its own container on your Mac. The original file isn't changed.
- **Logs.** The app writes operational messages to the macOS system log on your Mac. They aren't sent to the developer.

Deleting the app's container, `~/Library/Containers/com.acgso.timebreaker`, removes this data.

## No accounts, analytics, advertising, or tracking

Time Breaker has no accounts, analytics, advertising, or third-party code. It doesn't track you across apps or websites, and it doesn't connect to the internet on its own.

## Tips

Tips are optional in-app purchases. Apple processes them through the App Store under [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). The developer doesn't receive your name, Apple Account, or payment information. The app uses purchase information only to complete a tip, and doesn't store it or send it anywhere.

If you share analytics with app developers in System Settings, Apple may give the developer crash reports and usage statistics. These don't identify you.

## Children

Time Breaker doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
